Reading the board

Football sits beside whoever has the ball.
Red bars down both edges of a ticker game mean somebody's in the red zone. Your own game shows it as red down & distance up top.
Green bars mean one of your fantasy players is in that game. FINAL is green too.
Fireworks when your team scores a touchdown — yours only, not the opponent's.
Grey lines split your game from the ticker, and the two ticker games from each other.
Panel 64×64, 3mm pitch, 4096 RGB LEDs
Controller Seengreat ESP32-S3 HUB75, driven over DMA
Data ESPN scoreboard API — every 15 min pre-game, 5 sec live
Boards one firmware; the team is a one-line change before flashing
